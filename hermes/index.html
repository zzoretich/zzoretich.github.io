Hermes (personal)

A private assistant, not a public service.

Hermes (personal) is an assistant that one person runs on their own home server, for their own use. It doesn't accept sign-ups, and no one else's account is connected to it.

With its owner's consent, it reads the owner's Gmail and Google Calendar with read-only access, to summarise new mail, draft replies and suggest events. Drafts and suggestions go only to the owner, who decides what to send or add. It can't send, delete or change any mail or event.
