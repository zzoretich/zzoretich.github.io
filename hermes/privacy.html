Privacy policy

Hermes (personal) · updated 27 October 2026

Who uses it

Only its owner. Hermes (personal) is a private assistant running on the owner's own server. No one else can sign in, and no one else's Google account is connected.

What it can access

The owner's Gmail and Google Calendar, with read-only permissions only (gmail.readonly and calendar.readonly). It can't send, delete, label or change mail, and it can't create, change or delete events.

How the data is used

Only to give the owner what they ask for: summaries of mail, drafts of replies, and suggested events. These arrive as messages in the owner's private chat channel; the owner decides whether to act on them.

Where the data goes

Mail and calendar content is processed on the owner's server. The part needed to answer a request is sent to the AI model provider the owner uses (OpenAI), under the owner's account, with model training turned off, and the answer is delivered to the owner through a private Discord server only they belong to. The data isn't sold, used for advertising, used to train AI models, or shared with anyone else.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Hermes (personal)'s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Storage and deletion

The sign-in token and anything the assistant keeps stay on the owner's server, readable only by the account that runs it. The owner can delete them at any time.

Revoking access

Google Account → Security → Third-party connections → remove "Hermes (personal)". This ends access immediately.

Contact

Through the owner's GitHub profile, github.com/zzoretich.
